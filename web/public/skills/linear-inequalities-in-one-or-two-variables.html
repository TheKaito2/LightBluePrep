What this skill tests

Linear Inequalities in One or Two Variables asks you to write and solve inequalities, mostly from situations with a limit, such as a budget or a weight capacity. You find the greatest or least value allowed, test which points satisfy an inequality or a system, and read shaded regions on a graph. Remember to flip the sign when you multiply or divide by a negative number.

How it shows up on the SAT

Usually multiple choice: a word problem about a maximum or minimum, or a shaded graph.
