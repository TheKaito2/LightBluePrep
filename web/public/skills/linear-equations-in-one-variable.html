What this skill tests

Linear Equations in One Variable asks you to solve, build, and interpret equations such as 3(x − 4) = 2x + 7. You may solve for x, find the value of an expression without solving, decide whether an equation has one solution, none, or infinitely many, or turn a word problem into an equation. Clean algebra and a careful reading of what is asked matter most.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer, often a word problem about a rate, a fee, or a total.
