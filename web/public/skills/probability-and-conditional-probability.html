4 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Probability from counts in a story16 questionsCount the favorable outcomes and all outcomes from the words or list, then divide.
  • Probability from a table or frequency chart15 questionsFind the right cell or total in the table, then divide by the grand total.
  • Conditional probability (given that)13 questionsShrink to the row or column named after 'given/of those', then divide the matching count by that subtotal.
  • Find a count from a probability5 questionsMultiply probability by the total, or set up favorable/total = probability and solve for the unknown.
