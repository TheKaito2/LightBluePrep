What this skill tests

Probability and Conditional Probability asks for the chance of an outcome, usually from counts in a two-way table or a short story. In conditional probability, the words given or of those shrink the total to one row or column: divide the matching count by that smaller total. Other questions work backward from a probability to a count.

How it shows up on the SAT

A two-way table or a short description of counts, as multiple choice or a typed-in answer.
