What this skill tests

Inferences asks you to finish a passage with the conclusion that follows most logically from what it says. The text lays out facts, a study, or an argument and stops at a blank. Good answers use every clue in the passage and go no further. Wrong choices are often claims that sound true but that the text never supports, or that contradict a detail you skimmed past.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage ends in a blank, and you choose the completion that most logically follows from the text.
