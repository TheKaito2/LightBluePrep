5 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Work back from an outcome to its cause32 questionsAsk what produced the outcome, use any key fact the text gives, and pick the cause that fits every clue.
  • Carry a stated rule forward to its result32 questionsFind the rule or cause-effect link the text states, apply it to the case, and pick the result it forces.
  • Read what a study's result shows31 questionsSay what the researchers did and what they saw, then pick the claim that fits it and goes no further.
  • Weigh a claim against the evidence23 questionsFind the claim and the evidence or caveat against it, then pick the choice that follows the evidence.
  • Boil the stated facts down to one point22 questionsLine up the stated facts or examples and pick the claim that restates or generalizes them, adding nothing new.
