What this skill tests

Form, Structure, and Sense covers grammar inside a sentence: subject and verb agreement, verb tense and form, pronouns that match their nouns, plurals and possessives, and modifiers placed beside what they describe. The task is choosing the form that standard written English requires, which often means looking past the words that sit between a subject and its verb.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage with a blank; the choices are different forms of the same word or phrase.
