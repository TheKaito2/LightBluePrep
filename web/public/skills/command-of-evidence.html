6 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Complete the sentence with data from a table or graph93 questionsRead the right rows, bars or points, then pick the choice whose numbers match and make the claim true.
  • Pick the finding that supports a hypothesis69 questionsPredict what result would prove the claim, then pick the choice that links directly to it.
  • Pick the quote that illustrates a literary claim40 questionsFind the quote from the poem, novel or play that shows the exact idea in the claim, not just the topic.
  • Pick the data that supports or weakens a conclusion38 questionsWork out what pattern the conclusion needs, then find the choice describing it accurately in the table or graph.
  • Pick the real-world quote that backs a claim22 questionsChoose the quote from the scholar, article or respondent that matches every part of the claim.
  • Pick the finding that weakens a claim15 questionsFind the choice that contradicts the claim's logic or offers a rival explanation.
