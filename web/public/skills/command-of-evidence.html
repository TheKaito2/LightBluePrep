What this skill tests

Command of Evidence tests whether you can match a claim to the evidence that backs it. Sometimes the evidence is textual: a quotation from a poem or novel, or a finding from a study. Sometimes it is quantitative: a value or trend in a table or graph. Decide what the claim needs, then pick the one choice that supports or weakens it on every point.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage states a claim or hypothesis, and you choose the quotation, research finding, or data from a table or graph that fits it.
