What this skill tests

Linear Functions is about rules of the form f(x) = mx + b, given as an equation, a table, a graph, or a story. You evaluate the function, solve for an input, write the rule from two points or a situation, and explain what the slope and intercept mean in context: the slope is the change per unit, and the intercept is the starting value.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer, often with a table, a graph, or a pricing or growth story.
