What this skill tests

Words in Context tests vocabulary the way it is used, not as a list of definitions. You read a sentence or two with a missing word and choose the most logical and precise fit, or say what a word means in that passage. Signal words such as but, because, and for example point to the meaning before you look at the choices.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage with a blank or a word to define; the choices are single words or short phrases.
