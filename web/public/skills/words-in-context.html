5 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Predict the word from the nearby details82 questionsNo signal word: find the clue sentence, predict a simple word in your head, then match it to the choices.
  • Use the colon, dash or examples after it58 questionsA colon, dash, 'for example' or list explains the blank; choose the word that sums those details up.
  • Pick the word that fits a contrast clue50 questionsSpot but, although, while, instead or not; the blank is the opposite of (or sides against) the idea it contrasts.
  • Find what a word means in this passage40 questionsReread the sentence, guess your own meaning for the word, then match the choice that fits that exact use.
  • Pick the word that fits a cause or result clue31 questionsFind because, since, thus, so or given, then choose the word that makes the reason lead logically to the result.
