6 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Function of an underlined part, nonfiction46 questionsRead the sentences around it and name its job: example, evidence, contrast, definition, or counterpoint.
  • Main purpose of a nonfiction text30 questionsFind what the author is doing overall: reporting a study, arguing, describing, or explaining.
  • Main purpose of a story or poem excerpt24 questionsTrack what the narrator, character, or speaker does or feels, and pick the choice that captures it.
  • Overall structure of a nonfiction text22 questionsOutline the text part by part (claim, evidence, problem, example) and match the order of moves.
  • Function of an underlined part, story/poem20 questionsSee what the line adds to the character, mood, or situation around it, then name that role.
  • Overall structure of a story or poem7 questionsNote how the focus shifts from beginning to end (setting, event, reflection) and match that sequence.
