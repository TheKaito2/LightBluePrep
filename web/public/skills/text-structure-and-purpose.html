What this skill tests

Text Structure and Purpose is about what an author is doing rather than what the text says. Questions ask for the main purpose of a passage, how it is organized from start to finish, or the job one underlined sentence does, such as giving an example or raising a counterpoint. Outline the moves of the passage in a few words before you read the choices.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short informational or literary passage, followed by a question about its purpose, its overall structure, or the function of an underlined part.
