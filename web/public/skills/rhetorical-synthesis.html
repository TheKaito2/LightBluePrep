What this skill tests

Rhetorical Synthesis puts you in the place of a student writer. You get a set of bulleted notes and a goal, such as emphasizing a similarity or introducing a study to a new audience, and choose the sentence that uses the notes to meet that goal. Accuracy alone is not enough: the right sentence is the one that does exactly what the goal asks.

How it shows up on the SAT

A list of research notes and a stated goal; each answer choice is a sentence built from the notes.
