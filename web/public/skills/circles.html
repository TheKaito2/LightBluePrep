What this skill tests

Circles covers the equation of a circle, (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², arc length and sector area as fractions of the whole circle, radians, and tangent lines. You read the center and radius, complete the square to reach standard form, convert between degrees and radians with π/180, and use the right angle between a tangent and a radius.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer; nearly every question is rated medium or hard.
