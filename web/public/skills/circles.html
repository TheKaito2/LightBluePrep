8 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Read center and radius from the equation12 questionsMatch to (x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2, take h, k, and r as the square root of the right side.
  • Shift or resize a circle's equation9 questionsMove the center by changing h or k; scale r first, then square it for the right side.
  • Point, distance, or diameter with a circle9 questionsPlug the point in, use the distance formula, or take the midpoint of a diameter to get the center.
  • Arc length from central angle ratio9 questionsSet arc/circumference equal to angle/360 and solve for the missing piece.
  • Complete the square or expand the equation8 questionsGroup x and y terms, complete each square, then read the standard form or match the general form.
  • Convert between radians and degrees8 questionsMultiply by 180/pi for degrees or pi/180 for radians, then simplify.
  • Radii, tangents, and triangle facts7 questionsUse equal radii, tangent perpendicular to radius, and equal tangent segments in a triangle or quadrilateral.
  • Unit circle angles and trig values5 questionsReduce the angle by full turns of 2pi, then use the unit circle point for sin, cos, or tan.
