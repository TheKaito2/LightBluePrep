What this skill tests

Nonlinear Functions covers quadratic, exponential, and other nonlinear functions given as equations, tables, graphs, or models. You evaluate them, find vertices and zeros, interpret constants in context, build exponential growth and decay models, and work with shifts and equivalent forms. It is the largest Math skill in the question bank, so it rewards steady practice.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer, often a growth model, a projectile, or a parabola on a graph.
