What this skill tests

This skill covers equations that are not linear: quadratics, absolute values, radicals, and rational equations, plus systems where a line meets a curve. You solve by factoring, by the quadratic formula, or by substitution, rearrange formulas for one variable, and use the discriminant b² − 4ac to count solutions. Always check the answers for extraneous solutions.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer; the graphing calculator helps when a line and a curve cross.
