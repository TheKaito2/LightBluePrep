6 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Solve a quadratic by factoring or formula38 questionsMove terms to one side, then factor, use square roots or the quadratic formula; use sum/product of roots.
  • Rearrange a formula for one variable35 questionsUndo operations in reverse order (add/subtract, then multiply/divide) to isolate the target variable.
  • Count solutions or find a constant28 questionsUse b^2-4ac (or the vertex) to decide how many solutions exist or what constant gives one or none.
  • Solve a system by substitution27 questionsSubstitute one equation into the other, solve the resulting quadratic, then find the matching other value.
  • Solve abs-value, root, fraction equations26 questionsIsolate the special part, split, square, or cross-multiply, then check for extraneous solutions.
  • Read a system solution from a graph10 questionsFind where the described curve and line cross, then match that point to the answer choices.
