8 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Read a value, count, or range off a display21 questionsPull a frequency, minimum, maximum, or range from a table, bar graph, dot plot, or box plot.
  • Find the median of a list, table, or plot15 questionsOrder the values and take the middle one (or average the two middle ones).
  • Compare means or medians of two data sets13 questionsCompute or reason about center of each set (symmetry, frequencies), then compare.
  • Find the mean of a list or table11 questionsAdd all the values, then divide by how many there are.
  • Effect of adding, removing, or shifting values10 questionsDecide how mean, median, or range change when values are added, removed, or shifted.
  • Use a given mean to find a missing value7 questionsMean times count gives the total, then subtract known values to get the unknown.
  • Compare spread or standard deviation4 questionsJudge which set is more spread out from its mean; bigger spread means bigger SD.
  • Find the mean of combined groups3 questionsMultiply each group mean by its size, add the totals, divide by the total count.
