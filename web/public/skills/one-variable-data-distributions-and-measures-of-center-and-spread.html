What this skill tests

This skill covers describing one set of data. You find the mean and median from lists, tables, dot plots, and box plots, use a mean to find a missing value, and compare the center and spread of two data sets. A common question asks how the mean, median, or range changes when a value, such as an outlier, is added, removed, or shifted.

How it shows up on the SAT

A list, table, dot plot, histogram, or box plot, with multiple choice or a typed-in answer.
