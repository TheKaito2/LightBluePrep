What this skill tests

Lines, Angles, and Triangles covers angle facts and triangle relationships. You use the 180° triangle sum, vertical angles, linear pairs, and the angles formed when a transversal crosses parallel lines. You also set up proportions with similar triangles and decide which facts prove two triangles similar or congruent. Marking every known angle on the figure first is the habit that pays.

How it shows up on the SAT

Usually a figure with marked angles or sides, as multiple choice or a typed-in answer.
