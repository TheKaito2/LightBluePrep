What this skill tests

Boundaries covers the punctuation that marks where sentences, clauses, and phrases begin and end: periods, semicolons, colons, dashes, and commas. You decide whether two clauses can stand alone, whether an aside needs a matching pair of marks, and when no punctuation belongs at all. Most questions turn on spotting run-ons, comma splices, and sentence fragments.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage with a blank; the answer choices differ mainly in their punctuation.
