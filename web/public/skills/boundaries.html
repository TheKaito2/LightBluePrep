7 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Join two complete sentences63 questionsTwo full clauses: use period, semicolon, or comma + and/but/so; a comma alone is a run-on.
  • No punctuation needed45 questionsNever split subject-verb, verb-object, preposition-object, or an essential name or that-clause.
  • Set off extra info (paired commas, dashes)38 questionsIf you can remove the aside, mark both ends with matching commas, dashes, or parentheses.
  • Comma after an opening or closing phrase23 questionsPut a comma where an introductory or trailing phrase meets the main clause.
  • Introduce an explanation or list (colon)17 questionsAfter a full clause, a colon (or dash) can introduce an explanation, example, or list.
  • Punctuate items in a list14 questionsCommas between simple list items; semicolons if the items already contain commas.
  • Period or question mark at sentence end13 questionsDirect question (inverted verb) gets ?; statement or indirect question gets a period.
