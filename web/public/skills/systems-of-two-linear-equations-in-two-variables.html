8 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Substitute to solve for x or y26 questionsPlug the known value or expression into the other equation, then solve for the asked variable.
  • Pick the equation or count the solutions19 questionsCompare slopes and intercepts: same slope, different intercept means none; identical lines means infinite.
  • Find the unknown constant18 questionsMatch slopes and ratios for no/infinite solutions, or plug in the given point, then solve for the constant.
  • Find a combined value like x + y or 5x15 questionsSolve (or add/subtract the equations) to get x and y, then evaluate the requested expression.
  • Solve a word problem with two unknowns14 questionsDefine variables, write two equations from the story, solve, and answer what is asked.
  • Read the solution off a graph13 questionsFind where the two lines cross, or match the graph to its equations using points and slopes.
  • Choose the system for a word problem11 questionsName the two unknowns, then write one equation for the totals and one for the money or rates.
  • Add or subtract equations to eliminate10 questionsScale if needed, add or subtract the equations so one variable cancels, then solve for the other.
