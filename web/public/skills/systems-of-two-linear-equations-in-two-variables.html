What this skill tests

This skill covers two linear equations solved together. You solve by substitution or elimination, find a combined value such as x + y, decide whether a system has one solution, none, or infinitely many, and set up a system from a word problem. On a graph, the solution is where the two lines cross, and parallel lines mean there is no solution.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer; some give the system, others a word problem or a graph of two lines.
