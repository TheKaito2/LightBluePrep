What this skill tests

Central Ideas and Details asks you to read a short passage and say what it is mostly about, or to find a detail it states directly. Passages range from science and history writing to excerpts of novels and poems. The right answer restates the text closely: it covers the whole passage rather than one sentence, and it never claims more than the author does.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage, then one question about its main idea or one of its details, with four answer choices.
