6 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Main idea of an informational text50 questionsSay what the whole passage is mostly about, not one detail or an overreach.
  • Find a detail the text states29 questionsLocate the sentence that answers the question; the right choice restates it.
  • What a story or poem says about a person21 questionsUse the narrator's words and actions to decide what is true of the person or speaker.
  • Draw a conclusion the text supports19 questionsCombine clues in the text to find the one choice it implies; reject anything unstated.
  • Main idea of a story excerpt or poem14 questionsTrack what the narrator or speaker is doing or feeling across the whole excerpt.
  • What a researcher would likely agree with5 questionsFind the researcher's claim in the text; pick the choice that matches it, not extremes.
