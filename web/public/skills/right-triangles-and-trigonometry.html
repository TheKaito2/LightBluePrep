What this skill tests

Right Triangles and Trigonometry covers the Pythagorean theorem, the 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 special right triangles, and sine, cosine, and tangent as ratios of sides. You find missing sides, write trig ratios from side lengths, and use the fact that the sine of one acute angle equals the cosine of the other. Most of its questions are rated hard.

How it shows up on the SAT

Often a right-triangle figure or a description of one, as multiple choice or a typed-in answer.
