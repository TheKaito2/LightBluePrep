6 question types

Every question here is sorted by how you solve it. Pick one type and practice it until it sticks.

  • Plug numbers into a formula38 questionsGiven the dimensions, put them in the area, perimeter, volume or surface area formula.
  • Scale factor on similar or resized shapes22 questionsLengths scale by k, so perimeters scale by k, areas by k squared, volumes by k cubed.
  • Work backward to a missing side21 questionsGiven the area, volume or perimeter, set up the formula and solve for the missing length.
  • Chain two or more area or volume steps18 questionsFind one quantity first (side, radius, area), then feed it into a second formula or add/subtract.
  • Shapes on the coordinate plane6 questionsRead points, use distances or right-triangle pieces, then find the area or circumference.
  • Pick or build the right expression5 questionsWrite the formula with given values or variables and choose the matching expression.
