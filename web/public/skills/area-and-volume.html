What this skill tests

Area and Volume covers the perimeter, area, surface area, and volume of common shapes and solids, including rectangles, triangles, circles, prisms, cylinders, cones, and spheres. You plug into formulas, work backward from a given area or volume to a missing length, and use scale factors: when lengths scale by k, areas scale by k² and volumes by k³.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer, sometimes with a figure; the reference sheet lists most of the formulas.
