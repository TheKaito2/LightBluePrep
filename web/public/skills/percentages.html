What this skill tests

Percentages asks you to find a percent of a number, find the whole from a part, express one value as a percent of another, and work with percent increase and decrease. The key tool is the multiplier: a 15% increase multiplies by 1.15, a 15% decrease multiplies by 0.85, and several changes in a row multiply together rather than add.

How it shows up on the SAT

Short word problems about prices, populations, or survey results, as multiple choice or a typed-in answer.
