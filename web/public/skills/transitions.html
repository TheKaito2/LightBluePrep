What this skill tests

Transitions tests the logical link between two ideas. You read a short passage with a blank where a word or phrase such as however, therefore, or for example belongs, and choose the one that matches how the next sentence relates to the one before it: contrast, cause and effect, addition, example, sequence, or similarity. Name that relationship yourself before you read the choices.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short passage with a blank at the start of a sentence or clause; the choices are transition words and phrases.
