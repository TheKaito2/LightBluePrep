What this skill tests

This skill asks whether a conclusion is justified by how the data were collected. Results from a random sample apply only to the population it was drawn from. Only a randomized experiment supports a cause-and-effect claim, and a biased or nonrandom sample cannot support a general one. Read how the study was designed before you judge its conclusion.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short description of a study or survey and a choice of conclusions; it is the smallest Math skill in the question bank.
