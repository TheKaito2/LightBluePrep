What this skill tests

This skill is about what a random sample can tell you about a whole population. You scale a sample proportion or mean up to an estimate for the population, build a plausible range from an estimate and its margin of error, and reason about how sample size affects that margin: a larger random sample gives a smaller margin of error.

How it shows up on the SAT

A short description of a survey or sample, almost always with multiple-choice answers.
