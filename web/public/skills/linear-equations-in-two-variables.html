What this skill tests

Linear Equations in Two Variables covers equations such as 4x + 5y = 60 and the lines they draw. Questions ask you to write the equation for a situation, interpret a coefficient, find a slope or intercept, connect an equation to its graph or table, or use parallel and perpendicular slopes. Many are word problems with two kinds of items and a total.

How it shows up on the SAT

Multiple choice or a typed-in answer; graphs and short word problems are common.
