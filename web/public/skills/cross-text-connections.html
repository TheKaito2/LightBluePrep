What this skill tests

Cross-Text Connections gives you two short texts on the same topic and asks how they relate. You might predict how one author would respond to the other's claim, find the point both accept, or name where they disagree. It rewards a clear one-line summary of each text's position before you compare them, and a careful check that both halves of an answer fit.

How it shows up on the SAT

Two short passages, labeled Text 1 and Text 2, followed by one question about how they relate.
