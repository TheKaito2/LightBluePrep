What this skill tests

Two-Variable Data covers scatterplots, lines of best fit, and the models behind them. You read values from a graph, predict with the line of best fit, compare actual points with predicted ones, interpret a slope in context, and decide whether a linear, quadratic, or exponential model fits the data. Reading the axis labels and units carefully prevents most mistakes.

How it shows up on the SAT

A scatterplot or line graph, with a question about a value, a trend, or a model.
