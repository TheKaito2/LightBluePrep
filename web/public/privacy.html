Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

LightBluePrep is a free, non-commercial SAT practice site run by a student. You can use all of it without an account.

What we collect

No personal data, unless you sign in. There are no ads and no tracking cookies. Your answers, notes, highlights, marks and practice sets, and the topics and options you last picked on the Practice page, and whether you hid the practice timer, are saved only in your own browser's storage on your device.

Counting visitors

Two anonymous counts tell us whether anyone uses the site:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and records which page was viewed, the site that linked to it, how fast it loaded, the country, and the type of browser and device. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not fingerprint you, so it cannot tell one visitor from another.
  • Usage milestones. The first time your browser answers a question, and again when it has answered ten different questions, it sends our server one empty message, and the server adds one to that day's total. Your browser remembers in its own storage that it sent each one, so the message carries no ID, no cookie and nothing about you. All the server keeps is the daily totals. Deleting your data in Settings keeps that one note, so starting over does not count you twice.

Who your browser talks to

  • Cloudflare serves the website and our server (sync if you sign in, feedback, and the usage milestones), runs the Web Analytics described above, and like any web host it handles your IP address to deliver the page. Sign-in, feedback and the usage milestones are rate-limited to stop abuse, using your IP address only as a short-lived counter key; it is never stored.
  • College Board supplies the questions. Your browser downloads them directly from College Board; we never host or store question content.
  • Desmos supplies the calculator, and only if you open it.
  • Google handles sign-in. Its sign-in script loads only when you open the sign-in panel.
  • YouTube opens only if you tap the YouTube button on a question, which searches for that question's ID.
  • Ko-fi / Buy Me a Coffee handle donations, only if you follow a Support link. Their own privacy policies apply; we get nothing from them except the donation.
  • PromptPay donations go through your own Thai banking app when you scan the QR. The site never sees the payment.

If you send feedback

Bug reports and comments are stored anonymously on our server: the message you type, the question ID it is about (if any) and which screen you sent it from. No name, email, IP address or account is attached, so we cannot reply. Please do not put personal details in the message.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It uses Google sign-in so your progress follows you between devices. When you sign in, our server (a Cloudflare Worker and database) stores:

  • the questions you answered, your answers and how long they took
  • your highlights, notes, marks and mistake log
  • your practice sets

We never store your email, name or profile picture. Google gives us an anonymous account ID, and that ID is the only thing your practice history is linked to. Nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

  • This device. The site's Settings page shows what your browser has saved and has a button that permanently deletes it: your answers, marks, highlights and notes, mistake log and practice sets. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings also deletes your browser's copy, along with the questions it has cached.
  • Our server. If you signed in, a second button there (and in the sync menu, top right) permanently deletes everything stored on our server for your account.
  • Everything. You can delete either one alone, or both at once.

Children

LightBluePrep is meant for high school students preparing for the SAT. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on the GitHub repository, where all of the site's code is public.
